1. Executive Summary 
CargoMint is a cloud-native, multi-tenant logistics operating system purpose-built for African logistics 
companies. It is not a logistics company — it is the software infrastructure that logistics companies, fleet 
operators, and merchants run on. 
The platform brings together five distinct actor types — Logistics Tenants, Fleet Tenants, Merchants, 
Consumers, and Captains — into one coherent system where every actor can start and complete their 
workflows independently, without friction or dependency gaps. 
CargoMint is delivered as a white-label platform. Logistics companies operate under their own brand. 
CargoMint is the engine underneath, invisible to end users. 
 
2. Core Design Principles 
Every architectural and product decision must be tested against these principles before implementation. 
Principle What It Means 
End-to-End Actor 
Autonomy 
Every actor — Merchant, Consumer, Tenant, Fleet Tenant, Captain — must be able to 
start and complete their own workflows without being blocked by another actor or an 
incomplete screen. 
Tenant-Scoped 
Everything 
All operational data — shipments, staff, fleet, pricing, customers — is scoped to a Tenant. 
Nothing leaks across Tenant boundaries. The Super Admin has read access across all but 
does not own operational data. 
CargoMint is the 
System of Record 
All shipment data, delivery status, COD records, and actor relationships live in CargoMint. 
Third-party fleet software or partner integrations may read and write via API but CargoMint 
always holds the authoritative record. 
White-label by Default 
The platform is built for multi-brand operation. Consumers, Merchants, and Captains 
experience their Tenant's brand, not CargoMint's brand. CargoMint branding appears only 
at the platform discovery and Super Admin layer. 
Simple First, Scale 
Later 
Phase 1 solves the core logistics workflow cleanly. Marketplace features, automatic 
matching, and Partner API integrations are Phase 2. Complexity is added only when the 
network exists to justify it. 
No Floating Shipments 
Every shipment created on the platform must have a responsible Tenant assigned before 
it can be dispatched. A shipment cannot exist in a fulfilled state without a Tenant or Fleet 
Tenant accountable for its delivery.

4.1 Geography Hierarchy 
All operations — pricing zones, hub locations, routing — are anchored to this strict geographic hierarchy to 
support multi-country expansion from day one. 
• Country — top-level market with currency and locale metadata (e.g. Nigeria / NGN) 
• Region — named operational zone within a country (e.g. South-West, North-Central) 
• State — administrative sub-unit under a region. Hubs and stations map to states 
• Hub (Service Centre) — physical facility within a state. Manages inbound/outbound shipments 
• Station — sub-unit of a Hub for last-mile routing and captain assignment

7. Fleet Model 
Fleet management is handled differently depending on the Tenant type. The key principle is that captains 
always belong to a specific Tenant — they are not global freelancers hovering on the platform. 
7.1 Logistics Tenant Fleet Options 
When a Logistics Tenant is onboarded, they configure their fleet strategy in Settings. Three options are 
available: 
• Option A — Own fleet only: Tenant registers and manages all their own vehicles and captains. No external 
fleet used. 
• Option B — Own fleet and third-party fleet: Tenant uses own fleet for primary routes and contracts available 
Fleet Tenants for overflow, specialist routes, or markets where they lack coverage.  
• Option C — Third-party fleet only: Tenant operates as an asset-light logistics company. All delivery capacity is 
contracted from Fleet Tenants. 
This is a setting, not a new portal. It determines what appears in the manifest assignment flow. If Option B or 
C is selected, available Fleet Tenants are shown during dispatch — favourited Fleet Tenants displayed first.

8.2 How Logistics Tenants Charge — Freight Pricing 
Logistics Tenants set their own freight pricing using the Pricing Engine in the Operations Dashboard. The 
system calculates the total for every shipment automatically. 
Fee Component Type Detail 
Base Freight Zone x Weight 
matrix 
Price derived from weight bracket (kg) crossed against zone 
(A/B/C/D based on origin-destination distance). Tenant 
configures the matrix. 
Insurance % of declared value Optional. Applied to declared item value. Typically 1–1.5%. 
Fuel Surcharge % of base freight Covers variable fuel costs. Typically 6–10%. Configurable per 
Tenant. 
Handling Fee Flat per shipment Fixed fee covering packaging and processing at the hub. 
Type Surcharges % of base freight Express (+40%), Fragile (+25%), Bulky (custom). Applied on top 
of base freight. 
COD Fee % of COD value Charged to Merchant for cash-on-delivery collection and 
remittance service. Typically 1–2%. 
 
8.3 Merchant Contractual Rate Cards 
When a Merchant has a contractual relationship with a Logistics Tenant (via subscription/favourite with agreed 
rates), the Tenant creates a named Rate Card for that Merchant. Rate Cards override the standard pricing 
matrix automatically — no manual override needed at the desk. 
• Rate Cards are Merchant-specific — e.g. 'Konga Enterprise Rate' gives 15% off Zone B/C shipments 
• Rate Cards are managed in the Tenant's Pricing Engine settings

Fleet Tenants set trip-level fees per vehicle type — not per-shipment. They are selling capacity, not handling

shipments on that trip.

10. Open Items & Policy Decisions Required 
The following items are architecturally supported but require explicit policy decisions before development. 
They are flagged here to ensure they are not assumed or invented during implementation. 
ID Item Question / Decision Needed Status 
OI-01 COD Chain — Fleet Tenant 
delivery 
When a Fleet Tenant captain collects COD, the 
remittance chain is: Fleet Tenant → Platform → 
Merchant. Who bears liability if the Fleet Tenant fails to 
remit? What is the SLA for remittance? 
Policy 
OI-02 Merchant without Tenant — 
Phase 1 
In Phase 1, an independent Merchant must select a 
Logistics Tenant. What is shown if no Logistics Tenant 
covers their route? Is there a fallback or is the 
shipment blocked? 
Open 
OI-03 Fleet auto-matching logic 
Phase 2 auto-matching criteria need definition: 
distance, capacity, rating, price, availability window. 
What is the fallback if no Fleet Tenant accepts the job? 
Phase 2 
OI-04 Consumer scope — platform 
vs Tenant 
When a Consumer signs up on CargoMint directly, are 
they permanently platform-level or can a Tenant claim 
them as their customer if they always ship with one 
Tenant? 
Open 
OI-05 Pricing override approval 
workflow 
When an Ops user overrides shipment pricing, does 
this require a manager-role approval, or is it self-
approving with a reason code? Defines the compliance 
requirement. 
Policy 
OI-06 Multi-currency billing 
For Tenants operating across multiple countries: do 
they invoice in their home currency, or does CargoMint 
support multi-currency ledgers per Tenant? 
Open 
OI-07 Partner API spec for fleet 
software 
The handshake model with third-party fleet software 
needs a formal API spec defining: what CargoMint 
exposes, what it accepts from partners, and data 
ownership boundaries.

11. Phase Delivery Plan 
CargoMint is delivered in two phases. Phase 1 is the commercial SaaS core. Phase 2 is the logistics 
marketplace. Do not start Phase 2 until Phase 1 has live Tenants generating revenue. 
Phase Focus Deliverables 
Phase 1 SaaS Core — Logistics 
Operations 
Master Console, Operations Dashboard (full), Merchant 
Portal, Consumer App, Captain App. Per-shipment fee model. 
Logistics Tenant own-fleet management. Merchant carrier 
selection (manual). Platform Subscription billing. 
Phase 2 Marketplace — Fleet & On-
Demand 
Fleet Tenant Portal, fleet marketplace discovery, auto-
matching engine, Partner API spec for third-party fleet 
software integration, Fleet Tenant fee and settlement 
infrastructure, advanced analytics across all actor types.
